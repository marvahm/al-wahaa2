Our Story

Your Perfect
Oasis in the City

At Waha Cafe Lounge — الواحة — we believe that a great café is more than food and drink. It is a sanctuary. A place to pause, breathe, and truly savour the moment.

Drawing from the warmth of Middle Eastern hospitality and the vibrancy of London's café culture, every dish and every cup is crafted with intention. From slow-brewed morning coffees to indulgent brunch plates, every visit is a memory in the making.
